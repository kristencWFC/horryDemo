Use case 1 in detail: the payment hand-off

The member never reads a card number to a person, the agent keeps the call, and the call never leaves Webex Contact Center.

Set-up before the call (2 minutes). On the supervisor's AI Operations page (or the Storm Console, section 02), open the Demo caller card: put the presenter's 10-digit phone in Presenter phone, choose Dorothy Skipper under Plays the member, Save. That one setting makes both Savannah and the agent desktop treat your phone as Dorothy (Visa and ACH on file, $194 past due, cut-off 11/10); otherwise the desktop shows whichever member the phone was last set to. Then make sure AI agent · Savannah is off, log in as the billing agent in the other browser and open the Member tab. Dorothy dials (972) 903-8486 for this story. Switch the dropdown between calls to play Marcus or Linda; Reset demo data leaves it alone.
  1. Savannah is off. Dorothy calls (972) 903-8486. The flow hands the call to the WFC AI Controller, which brings her straight back into the contact center on (972) 430-9658 as herself; the billing agent answers and the Member tab pops her account: $194 past due, the due date in red.
  2. She wants to pay. The agent clicks Hand payment to Savannah. The service records the hand-off (member, agent, amount) under a 4-digit reference keyed to the caller's number.
  3. The controller puts the agent on hold (a short announcement, then music) and connects Dorothy to Savannah. Savannah already knows the member and the amount: "Dorothy, the agent asked me to take care of your payment of $194. Would you like to use the credit card on file, the ACH e-check on file, or a new credit card?" A new card is read to Savannah by voice, one item at a time; she repeats back only the last four digits. The agent hears none of it.
  4. The agent's Member tab shows each step as it happens: hand-off started, Savannah answered, payment approved with the confirmation number, sending the member back. The agent sees every step and hears none of the payment.
  5. Savannah says "I'll put you back with the agent now" and hangs up her side. The controller reconnects the agent and Dorothy on the same call: no wrap-up, no re-queue.
  6. The Member tab says "Back with you" and shows the payment. The agent finishes the conversation.

Use case 2 in detail: storm mode

Set-up (1 minute). AI Operations → AI agent · Savannah on (the Storm Console's switch is the same one). Demo caller → Linda Hardee (in the Socastee outage, Visa on file); for the wire-down variant switch to Marcus Jordan. Optional: tick KC_Horry_Billing under Outage team staffing so the billing agent also takes outage calls. Dial (972) 430-9658.
  1. The supervisor turns Savannah on. The flow's switch flips the same moment, and every call to the demo line goes to Savannah first.
  2. Linda calls. Savannah greets her by name (caller ID → account) and confirms the service address.
  3. "I see there is an outage in your area. Is this the reason for your call?" Yes: Socastee, about 1,240 members, a tree on the line, restoration in about 2½ hours.
  4. Savannah offers a text when the power is back (and another if the estimate changes). Linda says yes and is signed up. No agent rings. In the Storm Console, Mark restored sends the "power is back" text.
  5. Wire down: Marcus calls and says a line is down across his driveway. Savannah gives the 35-foot safety warning and connects him to a person at top priority; the agent's Member tab shows his account, the active outage and what Savannah already collected.
  6. Outage team staffing: one click adds a department's team to outage calls; Return to normal undoes it. Customer Review shows containment, why members called and each call's story.

Use case 3 in detail: payment reminders

  • A Payment reminders page for the supervisor: choose the CSV from the billing system (account, name, phone, amount, due date) or paste the rows, check and edit the list, choose Voice or Text, send.
  • Voice: Savannah calls each member in turn from the co-op's outbound number, 207-531-5136. "Hi, this is Savannah calling from Horry Electric Cooperative. Am I speaking with Dorothy?" Then the past-due amount and the same payment question: credit card on file, ACH e-check on file, or a new card by voice. If they can't pay today she offers an extension. Outcome per row: paid, promised to pay, no answer, declined.
  • Text: each member gets a reminder with a secure pay link; the row flips to "paid" when the link is used.
  • Customer Review shows every reminder as an outbound call or a text under the topic "Payment reminder".
For the demo, Send every row to the demo number points the whole list at the presenter's phone, so one person can answer as Dorothy.

Use case 4: web chat

Set-up. Open the demo co-op site in a browser the audience can see: https://kcdev2.workflowconcepts.com/horry/site.html. The chat bubble is bottom-right. The billing agent is logged in with the Web Chats page open. Savannah answers every chat first, whether or not she is on for calls.
  1. Click the bubble and start a chat as Linda Hardee (phone 843-555-0101): "Is my power outage known?"
  2. Savannah finds the account from the phone number and answers with the Socastee outage, the estimate and the offer of a text when power is back, exactly as she would on a call.
  3. Type "Can I talk to a person?" Savannah hands the chat over; the agent's Web chats badge at the top of the Desktop lights up.
  4. On the Web Chats page the agent accepts the chat and sees Savannah's part of the conversation and Linda's account alongside it, then replies in the same window the member is typing in.
  5. The chat shows on Customer Review under Web chat with its topic, path and sentiment, next to the phone calls.

Use case 5: email

Set-up. The agent opens the Email page on the Desktop. For the outage-group reply, Savannah on (storm mode). The board starts with twelve member emails from the last storm day.
  1. The board lists every email with who sent it, the topic and whether anyone has it. The agent picks one (or the supervisor assigns one to them from the same page).
  2. Draft reply writes the answer from the member's account: the outage at their address and its estimate, or the balance and due date, in the co-op's voice. The agent edits and sends; the reply is logged as a note on the account.
  3. With Savannah on, the board groups the emails about the Socastee outage; Draft one reply for N members writes a single message with each member's first name filled in, and one Send answers all of them and closes those emails together.
  4. An email that needs a person stays on the board until an agent takes it; nothing is auto-sent.
