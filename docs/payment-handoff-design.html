Payment Hand-off Design

The live agent hands Jamie to Savannah for the payment and never hears it. The WFC AI Controller sits in the middle of the call, holds the agent, sends Jamie to Savannah, and puts them back together on the same call. Members dial 972-903-8486; the controller brings them back in on 972-430-9658.
